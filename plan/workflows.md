AI Concierge seven-stage workflow process:

1. Patient intake: The patient or authorized healthcare proxy creates a case and uploads relevant documents, such as referrals, insurance policies, denial letters, medical notes, and explanations of benefits.
2. Document processing: The AI reads, categorizes, and organizes the documents, extracting important information such as dates, procedures, policy references, and authorization requirements.
3. Case understanding: The system reconstructs the patient's situation, creates a timeline of events, identifies outstanding issues, and determines what information is still needed.
4. Policy and denial analysis: The AI compares the available evidence against applicable insurance requirements, identifies potential inconsistencies or missing documentation, and explains the findings in understandable language.
5. Actionable case record: The system creates a central record containing the case summary, supporting documents, next steps, communications, deadlines, and outstanding tasks.
6. Action planning and follow-up: The AI prepares requests, appeals, correspondence, and follow-up activities. The patient reviews and approves consequential actions, while the system tracks progress and unresolved issues.
7. Patient delivery: The patient accesses a dashboard showing case status, progress, deadlines, correspondence, and recommended actions.
The key idea throughout is that AI Concierge manages an ongoing administrative case rather than simply analyzing a document or answering a question.
